# Recordings used by HearTabletTest

All three are 48 kHz, 16-bit, one channel, made from recordings on freesound.org.

| File | What it is | From | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tablet-out.wav` | A tablet squeezed out of a blister strip, twice. | "Tablet is squeezed out of the blister.wav" by tosha73, freesound.org sound 572319 | CC BY 4.0. Changed: made one channel, 48 kHz. |
| `busy-room.wav` | Eight seconds of a busy room, people talking in the background. | "Busy Room Ambience / People talking in background #2" by Breviceps, sound 465699 | CC0 |
| `people-talking.wav` | Eight seconds of a noisy pub. | "noisy_cambridge_pub.wav" by Zac_Gist, sound 457286 | CC0 |

They are test data only and are not part of the app.
